================================================================================
  Key Insights
================================================================================
  - ESP-NN Xtensa LX7 SIMD optimized Conv2D works (esp_nn_conv_s8)
  - 12x speedup is good but not the 40-100x expected theoretical max
  - Bottlenecks remain: Reshape, Concatenation, ResizeBilinear, non-ESP-NN ops
  - Some Conv2D layers may use dilation > 1 → fallback to reference ConvPerChannel
  - PSRAM bandwidth (80-100ns/access) still a limiting factor
  - No memory leak: PSRAM stable at 3.93 MB across all 17 inferences

    pooling.cc, softmax.cc, add.cc, mul.cc (replaced by esp_nn/ variants)
